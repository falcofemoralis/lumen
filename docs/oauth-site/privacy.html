Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

About Lumen

Lumen is an independent, open-source catalogue and media player for Android, Android TV and Windows. This policy describes the optional Google account and Drive synchronisation feature in this version.

Google account information

When you choose to connect, Google provides basic account information, such as your email address, account identifier and profile information, and OAuth tokens. Lumen uses this information to identify the connected account, display connection status and authorise synchronisation. Your Google password is entered only with Google; Lumen does not receive it.

Google Drive access

Lumen requests the drive.appdata permission to read and write its own application data folder. The feature uploads and restores application backup data, including your library and bookmarks, watch history and progress, selected settings and cached catalogue metadata. It does not request access to ordinary Drive files, documents, photos or other applications’ data.

Storage and sharing

App data is stored on your device. If you enable sync, backup data is also stored in your Google Drive account and processed by Google to provide that service. Windows OAuth tokens are protected locally with Windows DPAPI. A portable data folder should still be treated as private: application data and backup files are not all encrypted by Lumen.

The sync implementation sends Google account and backup data directly to Google APIs. It does not send that data to a separate Lumen cloud server. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Lumen’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

Browsing and playback send requests to the content sources you enable. Those providers may receive your IP address, requested titles and their own account or session data where you choose to sign in. Their processing is governed by their own policies. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard web request information.

Your controls and retention

Google sync is optional. You can disconnect in Lumen and revoke Lumen’s permission in your Google Account settings. Disconnecting stops synchronisation; it does not automatically delete previously uploaded backup files. You can remove hidden application data through Google Drive’s Manage apps settings. Local data remains until you remove it from the application or its data folder.
