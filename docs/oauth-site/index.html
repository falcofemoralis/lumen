Lumen

A media catalogue and player with Zona, HDRezka and Anixart sources. Browse titles, keep bookmarks, continue watching and organise your library across devices.

Optional Google Drive sync

Sign in with your Google account to synchronise your Lumen library, watch progress, selected settings and cached catalogue information. Lumen stores its backup in Google Drive’s private application data folder. It does not request access to your ordinary Drive documents or photos.

Sign-in uses Google’s authorisation screen. Lumen never asks for your Google password. You choose whether to connect and can disconnect in the app.

Open source

Lumen is an independent application. Content services and Google are not its publishers.
